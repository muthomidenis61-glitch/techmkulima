🇰🇪 Seasonal planning tips

🌧️
Rainfall first, calendar secondKenya's long and short rains differ by region and year. Check local forecasts and confirm soil moisture before planting.
🧪
Test soil before buying inputsUse soil-test results and crop recommendations to guide fertiliser and lime decisions; avoid guessing rates.
🌿
Scout weekly for pests and diseaseInspect both sides of leaves, stems, roots and nearby plants. Identify the problem before choosing a pesticide.
🧑🏽‍🌾
Use local extension supportAsk your county agricultural extension officer about locally recommended varieties, planting windows and approved crop-protection products.
