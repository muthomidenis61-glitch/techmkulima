AI Farm Assistant

Ask practical questions about Kenyan crops, livestock, feeding, soil, farm records and farm business. Signed-in farmers with active access can receive AI-generated guidance; if secure service setup is incomplete, MKULIMA will show an error rather than pretend it answered with AI.

Habari, mkulima! 🌱 I’m MKULIMA. What are you working on today? Ask about crops, animals, feed costs, farm planning or farm business.
Select a language first. / Chagua lugha kwanza.

Choose Kiswahili or English above to set the spoken answer language. / Chagua lugha hapo juu.

Safety first: AI guidance is an initial assessment, not a confirmed diagnosis or prescription. Verify locally before applying chemicals or treating animals. Serious animal illness, suspected contagious disease, poisoning, sudden deaths or rapidly spreading crop damage require qualified local assistance.

Crop & Animal AI Assessment

Describe the problem and optionally attach a clear crop, leaf, pest, animal or affected-body-part photo. When the secure AI service and your account access are active, MKULIMA sends the text and photo for AI-assisted first guidance. A photo alone cannot confirm a diagnosis.

Records remain on this device; cloud sync and notifications are not included.
Preparing for a veterinary visit? Create a structured animal history, mark emergency warning signs, record clinician examination findings, and print or export a case report. Open Veterinary Examination Record →
This is a record template, not an AI diagnosis or substitute for a qualified veterinarian.
Choose English or Kiswahili, speak, then review the text before submitting. / Chagua lugha, zungumza, kisha hakiki maandishi.
Animal Health & Treatment Records / Kumbukumbu za Afya ya Wanyama Keep animal/group health history, veterinary visits, vaccinations, treatments, follow-up dates and confirmed milk/meat withdrawal end dates. Export a backup from your phone. Open Animal Health Records →
Records are stored on this device. Follow a qualified veterinarian and product label; this tool does not diagnose or prescribe.
Urgent animal warning: breathing difficulty, inability to stand, severe bleeding, suspected poisoning, sudden deaths, or several animals becoming ill need prompt veterinary attention. Do not delay care while using an app.

About MKULIMA

MKULIMA™ is an agricultural technology concept created by AVENQOR HOLDINGS LIMITED. Our aim is to make useful farming information and digital farm tools more accessible.

This prototype is not a substitute for a qualified veterinarian, agronomist, soil laboratory or emergency service. AI features, cloud sync, authentication, subscriptions, notifications and marketplace transactions require further implementation and testing.
